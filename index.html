Cocovan &
the World Letter

In collaboration with artist Cocovan, this 3D animated narrative draws from Cocovan's journey in creating the World Letter- a 2500ft. participatory artwork connecting 145 countries through handwritten messages of love from the world to the world.

It was my Final Year Project for my undergrad during a period of global unrest. This short film is very personal to me and centers around hope, unity and women-lead creative resilience.
